# Generate Voucher Code

## When to use
A customer needs a voucher code generated through the agency portal.

## Steps
1. Open **Voucher Management** from the portal menu and select the **Generate Voucher Code** tab.
2. Complete the **Sender Information** step using the details supplied by the customer, following the on-screen stepper.
3. Select the account to be debited, enter the voucher amount and a motive, then click **Proceed**.
4. Review the transaction summary and fee. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, count and enter the quantity of each note and coin received. Check the calculated tally against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
5. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested.
6. When the success result displays the voucher code, copy it accurately and give it to the customer. Tell the customer to use it within its validity period.

## Common errors
- You opened cancellation when the request is to generate a code (or the reverse): stop and open the matching guide before proceeding.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
