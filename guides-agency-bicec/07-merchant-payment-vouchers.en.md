# Merchant Payment Voucher

## When to use
A customer needs help with a Merchant Payment Voucher.

## Steps
1. Open **Voucher Management** from the portal menu and select **Merchant Payment Voucher**.
2. Choose **Generate Voucher Code** to create a merchant cash-out voucher, or **Cancel Voucher Code** to cancel an existing code.
3. To generate a code, complete the **Sender Information** step. Select the account to be debited, enter the amount and motive, and fill in the recipient information requested on the form. Click **Proceed**.
4. Review the transaction summary and fee. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received, check the tally against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
5. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Copy the generated voucher code from the success result and give it to the intended recipient.
6. To cancel a code, enter the existing voucher code on the cancellation tab, click **Proceed**, verify the displayed voucher details, then enter the PIN and any requested OTP. Wait for the cancellation result.

## Common errors
- The customer is asking about voucher generation or cancellation rather than merchant payment: switch to the matching voucher guide.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
