# Outgoing Remittance

## When to use
A customer is asking about sending a remittance.

## Steps
1. Open **Remittance** from the portal menu and select the **Outgoing Remittance** tab.
2. Select the destination country and operator, then choose whether the recipient is receiving funds in an **Account**, **Wallet**, or **Card**.
3. Select the account to debit and enter the matching recipient account/card number or wallet ID.
4. Enter the amount and motive, then complete the recipient’s first and second names, ID type and number, address, city, country, and phone number. Complete the **Sender Information** step using the details supplied by the sender.
5. Click **Proceed** and review the recipient, destination, amount, and fee displayed by the portal.
6. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received, check the tally total against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
7. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Wait for the transaction result before starting another remittance.

## Common errors
- The customer wants to receive money, not send it: switch to Incoming Remittance before continuing.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
