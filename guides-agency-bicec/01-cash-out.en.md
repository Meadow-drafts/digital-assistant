# Cash-out

## When to use
A customer needs help with a cash-out, but has not said whether they mean Private/Internal or GIMAC.

## Steps
1. Ask whether the customer wants a **Private/Internal Cash-out** or a **GIMAC Cash-out**. Do not choose the workflow for them.
2. In the portal menu, open **Private Cashin - Cashout** for a private/internal transaction, or **GIMAC Cashin - Cashout** for a GIMAC transaction.
3. Select the **Cash-out** tab and check that the page heading and workflow match the customer’s request.
4. Continue with the matching guide: **Private Cash-out (Internal)** or **GIMAC Cash-out**. Follow its form fields and on-screen prompts in order.
5. Before you submit, check the voucher code, amount, and any displayed fee against the bank’s approved procedure. You remain responsible for the teller action; the assistant cannot fill or submit the form, execute the transaction, or approve a validation.

## Common errors
- The customer says only “cash-out”: clarify Private/Internal or GIMAC before opening a workflow.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
