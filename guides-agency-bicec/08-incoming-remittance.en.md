# Incoming Remittance

## When to use
A customer is asking about receiving a remittance.

## Steps
1. Open **Remittance** from the portal menu and select the **Incoming Remittance** tab.
2. Check that the tab is for receiving money sent to the customer, not sending a remittance. For a send, use the **Outgoing Remittance** tab and guide.
3. The current front-office source references an Incoming Remittance screen but does not include its form component. Follow only the fields and prompts actually shown in the signed-in portal; if the tab does not load or the next action is not clear, stop and contact authorized agency support rather than guessing.
4. Before completing any teller action, compare the displayed recipient and transfer details with the customer’s information and the bank’s approved procedure. Wait for the portal’s result and do not repeat an action with an unclear status.

## Common errors
- The customer wants to send money, not receive it: switch to Outgoing Remittance before continuing.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
