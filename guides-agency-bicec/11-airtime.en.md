# Airtime Topup

## When to use
A customer needs help with an airtime top-up.

## Steps
1. Open **Payments** from the portal menu and select **Airtime Topup**.
2. Select the destination country and mobile operator, then complete the **Sender Information** step as prompted.
3. Select the account to be debited. Enter and verify the customer’s phone number and enter the airtime amount.
4. Click **Proceed** and check the phone number, operator, amount, and displayed fee.
5. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received, check the tally total against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
6. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Wait for the result before attempting another top-up.

## Common errors
- The customer wants an internet bundle rather than airtime: switch to Internet Topup.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
