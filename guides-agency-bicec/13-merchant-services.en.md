# Merchant Services

## When to use
A customer needs help finding the agency portal’s merchant payment services.

## Steps
1. Open **Merchant Payments** from the portal or the merchant-services link.
2. Select the **Cash-in** or **Cash-out** tab to match the customer’s request.
3. For cash-in, select the country, transfer type (**Account** or **Wallet**), and operator in the stepper. Complete **Sender Information**, select the account to debit, enter the destination account/wallet, amount, and motive, then click **Cash-in**.
4. For cash-out, enter the voucher code and amount, then click **Cash-out** to look up the voucher.
5. Review the transaction details and fee. If a cash-denomination tally appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received, verify the tally total against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and confirm.
6. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Wait for the result before paying out cash or starting another transaction.

## Common errors
- The wrong tab is open: stop and select **Cash-in** or **Cash-out** to match the customer’s request.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
