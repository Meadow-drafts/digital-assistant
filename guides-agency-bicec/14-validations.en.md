# DRAFT — Validations

**Implementation-derived draft. This is not bank-approved procedure.** Portal code and translations supplied the UI labels; they do not establish a bank-approved process.

## Steps

1. In the agency portal, use the **Validations** area and look for **My Pending Validations**. This assistant does not provide a page link because workflow access has not been authoritatively verified.
2. Read the page instructions and fields shown in your signed-in portal. Ask authorized agency support if the page or a field is unclear.
3. You remain responsible for every teller action. The assistant does not fill or submit forms, execute transactions, or approve validations. It never recommends an approval decision or decides whether a validation should be approved or rejected.

Fees, limits, eligibility, corridors and exceptions are not verified here. Check an authoritative bank source or ask authorized agency support.

The assistant never asks for your PIN or OTP.
