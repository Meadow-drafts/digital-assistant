# Validations

## When to use
A teller needs to review the pending validations list.

## Steps
1. Open **Validations** from the portal menu.
2. Select **My Pending Validations** to see items awaiting your action. Check each row’s service, description, wallet, maker, and validation level.
3. Select the row’s view/details control to inspect the full request. Compare its details with the source information and the bank’s approval procedure.
4. Only if your role and bank procedure authorize it, choose **Approve** or **Deny** and confirm that choice in the dialog. This guide does not recommend whether to approve or reject any request.
5. Check the refreshed list or status to confirm the portal recorded the action. If the request or status is unclear, do not guess; contact authorized agency support.

## Common errors
- An expected item is not listed or its status is unclear: do not infer its status or make an approval decision. Ask authorized agency support.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
