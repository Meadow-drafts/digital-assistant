# Bill Payment

## When to use
A customer needs help with paying a bill through the agency portal.

## Steps
1. Open **Payments** from the portal menu and select **Bill Payment**.
2. Select the destination country and biller/operator, then complete the **Sender Information** step as prompted.
3. Select the account to be debited. Choose **Bill Number** or **Contract Number**, enter the matching reference, and click **Proceed**.
4. In the bill inquiry, review the listed bills. Select the checkbox for each unpaid bill the customer wants to pay, then click **Validate**.
5. Check the selected bill references and total. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received, check the tally total against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
6. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Wait for the payment result; do not submit the same bill again while its status is unclear.

## Common errors
- The customer is asking about airtime or internet rather than a bill: switch to the matching guide.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
