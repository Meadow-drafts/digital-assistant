# GIMAC Cash-in

## When to use
The customer needs help with the GIMAC cash-in workflow.

## Steps
1. Open **GIMAC Cashin - Cashout** from the portal menu, then select the **Cash-in** tab.
2. Select the destination **Country**, then select **Account** or **Wallet** under **Transfer Type**.
3. Select the destination **Operator** and complete the **Sender Information** step using the details supplied by the sender. Follow the stepper prompts to reach the transaction form.
4. Select the account to be debited. Enter the destination account/IBAN or wallet ID, the cash-in amount, and a motive (at least three words).
5. Click **Cash-in**. Review the transaction summary and confirm that the country, operator, destination, amount, and fee are correct.
6. If **Cash Denomination Tally** appears, enter the quantity of each note and coin received. The portal multiplies each quantity by its denomination and calculates a tally total. Check it against the target amount, enter the collected amount, and click **Confirm and Deposit**.
7. Enter the transaction PIN in the portal and provide the OTP there if requested. Wait for the transaction result before starting another transaction.

## Common errors
- The page is on the Private workflow: stop and switch to GIMAC. Do not continue in the wrong workflow.
- A field is missing, disabled, or rejected: read the portal’s message and check the relevant information. Do not invent a value; ask authorized agency support if the message does not explain what to do.
- The result is unclear or has not appeared: do not repeat the action. Check the status in the portal or ask authorized agency support.

This guide does not confirm fees, limits, eligibility, corridors, or exceptions. Check an approved bank source for those details.

The assistant never asks for your PIN, OTP, or another authentication secret. Do not share one in chat.

## Talk to a human if
The portal does not explain what to do next, a status remains unclear, or the customer needs help beyond these steps. Use the Human-agent option and review and edit the case summary before handoff.
