# Pay a bill

## When to use
You want to pay a utility or service bill from your account.

## Steps
1. Open **Payments**, then **Bill payment** ("Perform a Bill payment").
2. Choose the **country**, then the **biller**. Some billers may be locked by your plan.
3. In the right-hand panel, choose the account to debit.
4. Under "Select Bill Type", choose **Bill Number** or **Contract Number**, then enter that number.
5. Enter the **Amount** and the **Motive** if the form asks for them.
6. Click **Proceed**. The portal looks up the bill and shows a "Transaction Confirmation" table: bill reference, due amount, date and status.
7. Tick the bill(s) you want to pay (only unpaid bills can be ticked), then click **Validate**.
8. Enter your PIN. If your profile requires it, enter the OTP sent to you.
9. Wait for the result: **Payment Successful**, **Payment Pending** or **Payment Failed**. Keep the transaction reference.

## Common errors
- No bill listed: re-check the bill or contract number with your biller.
- Nothing can be ticked: the bill is already paid.
- "Insufficient funds": the due amount is above the account balance.
- **Payment Pending**: do not pay again. Check **History** first; paying twice can charge you twice.

## Talk to a human if
You were debited but the biller says it is unpaid, or you paid twice.
