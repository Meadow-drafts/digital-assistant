# See your cards and their status

## When to use
You want to see which cards you hold, check a card's status (for example whether it is active, blocked or in production), or find the claims link for a card.

## Steps
1. Open **Consultations** (the page is titled **Accounts and Cards**).
2. Open the **Cards** section.
3. Each row shows **Card reference**, **Card type**, **Truncated card number**, **Category** (Personal or Company), **Renewable** (Yes or No), **Creation date** and **Card status**.
4. In the **Action** column, the icon titled **Claims** opens the bank's claims page in a new tab.

## Card status
The portal shows one of these in **Card status**: Card requested, Card rejected, Card request validated, Card request sent to production, Card in production, Card laminated in agency, Card laminated outside agency, Card laminated sent to destruction office, Card received in agency, Card given to client, Active card, Card in opposition, Blocked card, Cancelled card, Expired card, Card under refurbishment.

## Common errors
- The table is empty: the portal has no card on record for your profile.
- The **Cards** section is view-only. The portal cannot request, block, unblock or replace a card, set a card limit, or show card transactions (see the list of things the portal does not offer).

## Talk to a human if
A card's status looks wrong, a card is missing, or you want to block, replace or dispute a card.
