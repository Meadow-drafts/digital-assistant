# Read your notifications

## When to use
You want to see alerts about your account and operations.

## Steps
1. Open **Notifications** from the bell icon in the header or the menu.
2. The page "All Notifications" lists them grouped by day (Today, Yesterday, then dates). Each shows a title, the date and the time.
3. The list is read-only: you cannot reply or delete from it.

## Common errors
- Empty list: there is nothing new yet.

## Talk to a human if
You expected a notification about a transaction that is missing, or you see one you do not recognize.
