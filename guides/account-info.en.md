# See your accounts, balance, RIB/IBAN and account actions

## When to use
You want to see your accounts, check a balance, get your RIB or IBAN, or jump to history, a transfer or a claim for an account.

## Steps
1. Open **Accounts** (Consultations). Tabs: **My accounts**, **My deposits**, **Cards**, **Loans**, **Other products**.
2. In **My accounts**, each row shows the branch, account number, key, title, account balance and available balance. Balances may need your PIN to be revealed.
3. Use the icons on the row: **RIB & IBAN** (view details), **Download as PDF**, **History**, **Transfer to wallet**, **Transfer to** the bank's own account, and **Claims**.

## Common errors
- An icon or tab is locked: not in your plan.
- Balance not shown: reveal it with your PIN; it never goes through the chat.

## Talk to a human if
A balance or account looks wrong, an account is missing, or you want to file a claim.
