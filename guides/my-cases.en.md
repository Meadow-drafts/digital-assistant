# Follow your cases

## When to use
You want to check a request you sent to the support team.

## Steps
1. Open **My cases** in the assistant.
2. Your cases are listed newest first. Each case shows its reference, when it was opened, its state and (except email-only cases) its last update.
3. Select a case to see its current state.
4. If the case is still open, click **Back to this chat** to return to that conversation. Closed cases do not have this button.
5. An **Emailed to support** case cannot be tracked here; its entry says so.

## Common errors
- **Sign in to see your cases.** Sign in to the portal, then open **My cases** again.
- **We could not load your cases.** Try again. If the problem continues, contact a human agent.

## Talk to a human if
Your case is missing or its state does not look right.
