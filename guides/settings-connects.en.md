# Change which connect is the primary connect

## When to use
Several wallets (connects) are linked to your account and you want to choose which one is the primary connect. The primary connect sees Account History and bulk history.

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, tab **Manage connects**. (If you do not see it, you are not the primary connect.)
2. The page shows the **Current primary** connect and the other connects.
3. Select the connect to promote, then click **Assign primary**.
4. You should see "Primary connect updated." If you are already primary, the page says "You are already the primary connect."

## Common errors
- Tab missing: only the primary connect can manage connects.

## Talk to a human if
You lost access to the primary connect, or you cannot reach Account History.
