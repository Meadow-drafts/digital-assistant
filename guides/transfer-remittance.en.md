# Send an outgoing remittance (money to someone who may not have an account)

## When to use
You want to send money to a person identified by account, wallet, card or voucher, with their identity details. This is also how to send money to another country, including elsewhere in the CEMAC region.

## Steps
1. Open **Transfers**, tab **Outgoing Remittance**.
2. Under "Type of Outgoing Remittance", pick **Account**, **Wallet**, **Card** or **Voucher**. A locked card is not in your plan.
3. Choose the account to debit.
4. Enter the receiver's **Account Number**, **Wallet ID** or **Card Number** (depending on the type), then the **Amount** and **Motive** (at least 3 words).
5. Fill the receiver's identity under "Recipient info": **First Name**, **Second Name**, **ID Type** (National ID Card, Passport or Refugee's Card), **ID Number**, **Address**, **City**, **Country**, **Phone**, **Date of Birth**.
6. Click **Proceed**, check the confirmation window, then enter your PIN (and the OTP if your profile requires it).

## Common errors
- Missing identity field: all are needed.
- "Insufficient funds".
- Wrong PIN or OTP.

## Talk to a human if
The recipient cannot collect the money, or you entered the wrong identity or number.
