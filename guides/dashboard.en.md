# Overview: see your balance and spending

## When to use
You want to see your account balance, available balance and spending statistics.

## Steps
1. Open **Overview** (you land here after login).
2. Choose the account ("Select account"). The account number and name are shown.
3. The balance is hidden. Click the eye icon, and enter your PIN if asked, to show **Account Balance** and **Available Balance**. The balance hides itself again after about 30 seconds.
4. Use the period buttons **Today**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Yearly** to change the statistics shown.

## Common errors
- Balance locked or an upgrade message: balance viewing is not in your plan; contact your branch.
- Wrong PIN: retype it. Never type your PIN in the chat.

## Talk to a human if
The balance looks wrong, or you cannot reveal it even with the right PIN.
