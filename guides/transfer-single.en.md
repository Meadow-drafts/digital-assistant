# Send a single transfer

## When to use
You want to send money once, to an account or a wallet, locally or through GIMAC.

## Steps
1. Open **Transfers** in the menu, then the **Single Transfer** tab.
2. Choose where it goes: the bank's own network (default) or **GIMAC**. For GIMAC, also pick the country and the operator.
3. Under "Type of single transfer", pick **Account** or **Wallet**. If a card shows as locked, your plan does not include it.
4. In "Transfer Information":
   - **Account**: choose **Myself** (then pick one of your accounts) or **Another person** (then enter the account number, as **IBAN** or **Simple**).
   - **Wallet**: enter the **Receiver Wallet**. You cannot send to your own wallet.
5. Enter the **Amount** and the **Motive** (at least 3 words).
6. Click **Proceed Transfer**. The "Transaction Confirmation" window shows the recipient, amount and fee. Check them carefully.
7. Enter your PIN and click **Validate**. If your profile requires it, you will then be asked for the OTP sent to you.
8. Wait for the result: **Payment Successful**, **Payment Pending** or **Payment Failed**.

## Common errors
- "Insufficient funds": the amount is above your balance.
- "Wrong PIN code" or "Wrong OTP code": retype carefully; do not share them with anyone.
- "Destination wallet/Account was not found": check the number with the recipient.
- Limit messages (daily, weekly, monthly): you reached a limit; the message shows the amount and the maximum.
- **Payment Pending**: do not send it again. Open **History** and check the status first.

## Talk to a human if
You sent money to the wrong person, a payment stays pending, or you were debited but the transfer shows failed.
