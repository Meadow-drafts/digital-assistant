# Buy airtime

## When to use
You want to top up a phone with credit from your account.

## Steps
1. Open the **Airtime** entry in the menu (page "Value Added Services"), on the **Airtime** tab. (**Bulk Airtime recharge** is the tab for many numbers at once.) If the tab is locked, your plan does not include it.
2. Choose the **country**, then the **operator**. If you see "No Operators Available", operators are not yet available for that country.
3. In the right-hand panel, choose the account to debit ("Select account to be debited from"). Its balance is shown.
4. Enter the **Phone** number to top up and the **Amount**.
5. Click **Proceed**. A "Transaction Confirmation" window appears. Check the details.
6. Enter your PIN and validate. If your profile requires it, enter the OTP sent to you.
7. Wait for the result: **Payment Successful**, **Payment Pending** or **Payment Failed**.

## Common errors
- "Insufficient funds": the amount is above the balance of the chosen account.
- The fields stay disabled until you pick an operator.
- "Wrong PIN code" / "Wrong OTP code": retype carefully; never share them.
- **Payment Pending**: do not buy again. Check **History** first.

## Talk to a human if
You were debited but the phone was not topped up, or the payment stays pending.
