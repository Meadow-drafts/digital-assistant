# Cardless withdrawal

## When to use
You want to withdraw cash without a card, for yourself or for another person, or to cancel a request you made.

## Steps
1. Open **Cardless Withdrawal**. Three tabs: **Myself**, **Another person**, **Cancel request**. A locked tab is not in your plan.
2. **Myself** or **Another person**: choose the account to debit, check the **Phone** number (it is your own number and fixed under "Myself"; enter the person's number under "Another person"), and enter the **Amount**.
3. The amount must be in multiples of 5 000. This rule applies to cardless withdrawal only.
4. Click **Proceed**, then enter your PIN (and the OTP if your profile requires it).
5. A withdrawal code is displayed. Copy or note it and keep it private: whoever holds it can withdraw.
6. **Cancel request**: enter the code you were given, then confirm with your PIN. Once cancelled it can no longer be used.

## Common errors
- "Amount should be in multiples of 5000".
- "Insufficient funds".
- Wrong PIN or OTP.

## Talk to a human if
You lost or shared the code by mistake, were debited without receiving cash, or cannot cancel.
