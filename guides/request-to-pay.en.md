# Request money, and confirm a request you received

## When to use
You want someone to pay you (Request to Pay), or you received a request and want to pay it.

## Steps: send a request
1. Open **Request to Pay**, tab **New Request**.
2. Choose the **country**, then the **operator**.
3. In "Request Information", enter the **Receiver Wallet** (the person you are asking), the **Amount** and the **Motive** (at least 3 words).
4. Click **Send Request**, check the "Transaction Confirmation" window, enter your PIN (and the OTP if your profile requires it).
5. You should see "Your payment request has been sent successfully".

## Steps: follow or cancel a request you sent
- **Pending Request** tab: your open requests, with their ID, wallets, date, amount and status. Click **Cancel** on a row and confirm to withdraw it.
- **Request History** tab: all past requests.

## Steps: pay a request you received
1. Open **Payments**, tab **Confirm Request to Pay**.
2. Find the request (ID, sender wallet, amount, date, status) and open it.
3. Check the amount and recipient, enter your PIN "to confirm Request to pay".

## Common errors
- "Wallet not found": check the receiver wallet.
- The send button stays disabled until the operator, wallet, amount and motive are filled.
- Tabs locked: your plan does not include Request to Pay; contact your branch.

## Talk to a human if
You paid a request you did not recognize, or you were debited twice for the same request.
