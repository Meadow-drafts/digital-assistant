# Log in, and what to do when you cannot

## When to use
You cannot log in, your account is locked, or you are asked to update your password or PIN.

## Steps: log in
1. On the login page, enter your **Wallet ID** and your **Password** (the field is labelled Password, not PIN).
2. Complete the **Security Check**: type the code shown (use **Refresh code** if it is unreadable).
3. Click **Login**. If your profile requires it, enter the OTP sent to you.
4. You arrive on **Overview**.

## First login or expired credentials
- First login: the portal asks you to initialise your password, then your PIN.
- "Your password has Expired, Please update your password" or "Your Pin has Expired, Please update your pin": enter the old value, the new one and confirm it. A new password needs at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter and one special character, 8 to 15 characters.
- A message "Pins do not match" or "Passwords do not match": retype the confirmation.

## Messages and meaning
- "Your account is inactive": the account is not active.
- "Number of connection attempts exhausted: Your account has been deactivated" or "more than three attempts": the account was deactivated after failed attempts.
- "Please wait about 2 minutes before trying again": wait, then retry.
- "You have not subscribed to this service" or "Your subscription is not yet effective": the subscription is not active.
- "Your account is waiting to be linked at the bank level" / "Please validate account attachement": the account link is pending.
- "Code doesn't match, try again": the security check code was wrong.

## Talk to a human if
Your account is deactivated or inactive, you forgot your password or PIN (the portal has no self-service reset), or the subscription or link is not active. The assistant cannot reset anything and never asks for your password, PIN or OTP.
