# Get an account statement

## When to use
You need a statement for an account over a period, as a PDF or by email.

## Steps
1. Open **History**, then **Account History**. If you do not see it, you are not the primary connect (see Settings > Manage connects).
2. Select the account, then open the **Account statement** tab.
3. Choose the period: **Last 30 days**, **Last 60 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Custom date** (pick a **Start date** and an **End date**). Maximum range: 90 days.
4. Click **Submit** to display the statement.
5. Click **Download PDF**, or enter your **Email** and click **Send statement**.

## Common errors
- "Maximum range: 90 days": shorten the dates.
- The end date must be after the start date.
- Menu or tab locked: your plan does not include it; contact your branch.

## Talk to a human if
You need a statement older than what the portal gives, or a figure on the statement looks wrong.
