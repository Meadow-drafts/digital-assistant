# Merchant tools: cash in, cash out, generate a QR code, consume a voucher

## When to use
You are a merchant client and need to take payments or serve customers.

## Steps
- **Cash In** (menu "Cashin - Cashout", tab **Cash In**): choose the account to debit, enter the beneficiary **Account Number** or **Wallet ID**, the **Amount** and the **Motive**, then **Proceed** and confirm with your PIN.
- **Cash Out** (tab **Cash Out**, shown only if your merchant profile has an alias): enter the **Receiver Wallet**, **Amount**, **Motive** and the recipient's identity (names, ID type and number, address, city, country, phone, date of birth), then **Proceed** and confirm with your PIN.
- **Generate QR Code** (merchant payment menu): enter the **Amount** and **Motive**, **Proceed**, confirm with your PIN, then **Download QRCode** to show or print it.
- **Consume voucher**: enter the voucher code the customer gives you and the required details, then confirm with your PIN. The code must be valid and unused.

## Common errors
- Cash Out tab missing: your profile has no merchant alias.
- Invalid or already used voucher code.
- Locked tab: not in your plan.

## Talk to a human if
A customer paid but you did not receive it, or a voucher is refused although the customer says it is valid.
