# Change your PIN

## When to use
You want a new transaction PIN, and you know your current one. If you forgot it, see "Talk to a human".

## Steps
1. Open **Settings**, then the **Change Pin** tab.
2. Enter your **Current Pin**.
3. Enter your **New Pin**. Its length must match the PIN length set for your profile.
4. Under "Confirm your new pin", type the new PIN again.
5. Click **Proceed**. You should see "Pin Updated Successfully".

## Common errors
- "Pins do not match": retype the new PIN and its confirmation.
- Wrong current PIN: retype it. Several wrong attempts can lock your access.
- The assistant never asks for your PIN or OTP and you should never type them in the chat.

## Talk to a human if
You forgot your PIN, your PIN expired and you cannot log in, or your access is locked.
